# Complexes (CX1-CX16, decision 0147)

A named place made of other places, on the area card and map and in a moment's arrival line. Information only. `lib/complexes/` (`rule.ts` core; `shell.ts`, `overtureReader.ts`, `index.ts` shells; `overtureSource.ts` the adapter that alone knows Overture's `buildings` and `places` tiles); the spec is `specs/complexes/spec.md`.

- **Judged on real data.** `scripts/fixture-complexes.mjs` saves the named buildings and the places inside them from Overture tiles (Harrisburg, Whitehall); `rule.test.ts` runs the rule on them. Tune `MIN_GO`, `MIN_MARKET`, `MIN_CAMPUS`, `MIN_CIVIC` there and bump `COMPLEX_CODE`. In dense downtowns many named buildings hold places (a bank building held 24, mostly offices): Shopping counts only places to go (presence weight 1), never offices or services.
- **Tiles carry a buffer, so a building is in more than one.** Strawberry Square lies whole in one z14 tile with a 3-point fragment in the next; the Lehigh Valley Mall is cut across two. Pieces are joined by the building's Overture id, and a building is judged only when every tile that holds a vertex of any of its pieces is read (`neededTiles`); one not fully read is missing, never a partial outline. The shell keeps pieces and places by tile for the session (`tiles`), so a repeat asks for nothing.
- **Kinds come from the building's type and what it holds** (the owner's call, 0147): markets by two market-category places; stations by `transportation` subtype and a class that is not parking; campuses by `medical` or `education`; civic by `civic`; shopping by contents. First match wins. A courthouse that holds eight places is Civic.
- **Viewing a moment never fetches** (CX12): the resurface and gallery shells ask `db.complexesNear` and `complexAt`; only `useComplexes` (the selected area on Explore) reads tiles, silently, and a failure is asked again after 90 s, by its timer alone (no render to set it). On Explore it starts once the camera is still (`stillness`, smooth-switches SS9).
- **Never counted, ranked or marked.** The card's rows are name, kind word and Walk to; nearest first, then name, then id; "See more" has no number; the map layer's labels are name and kind word only. `notMark`, `notVisited` and `surfaces` tests hold the invariants.
- **The e2e world:** `complexWorld.serve` (harness) serves the Wickenden Street Arcade and Public Market Hall at Transit Street (a long name on purpose; a building and nine restaurants near Fox Point) only while the `complexes` journey runs; every other journey gets an empty `buildings.pmtiles`.
- **Native** (unverified): the outline source and the Markers, migration 23. See `docs/NATIVE_UNVERIFIED.md`.
- **Audit rules (2026-11-12, complexes audit 1-28):** a place near a tile's edge is in both tiles' buffers: count it once, by Overture's `id`, and only once it is filed under its own tile (one seen first from a neighbour's buffer, with its own tile not yet read, is left for that read). A read says `failed` (nothing came: asked again after `COMPLEX_RETRY_MS` by a timer) apart from `partial` (the budget cut the tiles, a wanted tile did not read, or a building needs a tile never read). A tile that fails is left unread; the others stand. Rows of an older `COMPLEX_CODE` are ignored by `complexAt`. A station is a `transportation` building whose class says so (`transportation`, or a `station` or `terminal` in it); no class is no station. The minimums are a gate for "made of other places" and are never shown or used to sort (0103); Campuses and Civic count places of any class, Shopping places to go. The gallery shows street names only; a complex reaches only the arrival line (Memories, and the viewer, which shows it). With every kind switched off nothing is read. The outline is deep plum (`#4a044e`) because every kind and layer colour is taken: `audit 24` keeps it 55 apart in RGB.
