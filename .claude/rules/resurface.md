# Resurfaced moments (Chronicle's Memories)

Chronicle opens on Memories, a journal since decision 0155 (`.claude/rules/journal.md`): one resurfaced moment as a slip on today's page, the threads the user keeps coming back to (behind the contents), and every moment as an entry. The spec is `specs/resurfaced-moments/spec.md` (RM1-RM25); the owner's calls are in `docs/decisions/0098-chronicle-opens-on-memories.md`. The codemap is in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.

- **Nothing is a score.** Threads are ranked internally (`Thread.score`), and nothing ranked, weighted or counted is ever shown. Every sentence comes from `lib/resurface/lines.ts`, which writes numbers as words; a test checks no digit leaves it (RM22). Dates and a memo's length are the only digits on Memories.
- **Signals are the extension point** (`types.ts`, `extractors.ts`). `deriveThreads`, the feed and the shell read `Signal`s and nothing else. A transcriber fills a memo's `MomentView.words` (through `ResurfaceOptions.transcript`), or a new `SignalExtractor` is added to `DEFAULT_EXTRACTORS`; neither touches the feed. Never read `Capture` fields in `threads.ts`, `pick.ts` or `feed.ts`.
- **Not the owner's threads.** Owner-made threads (`lib/threads`, decision 0104) are stored and separate: this module never imports them, and they never import this one. A Chronicle thread can be copied into one with **Keep as my thread**.
- **Derived, never stored** (RM23). Threads are recomputed from the captures each time Memories reads them; the only write is meta `resurface_prefs` (ids and terms, never text from a moment). A derivation rule change bumps `THREAD_RULES`.
- **Pure core, one shell.** `compose` takes the clock, the seed and the hidden ids as arguments. A delete runs it again (the view passes `useHidden`), so a thread under three moments leaves the rail at once (RM19).
- **The top card must not reshuffle itself.** `prefs.lastShown` changes as soon as a card is shown; the order is composed with `state.lastShownAtOpen`, fixed at load (RM8).
- **A thread is a term in three moments on two days** (`THREAD_MIN_MOMENTS`, `THREAD_MIN_DAYS`), and not one in more than 40% of all moments once there are ten (`THREAD_MAX_SHARE`). English only: the stop-word list is in `terms.ts`.
- **Chronicle opens on Memories** (RM1): `chronicleSection` (store) is told of every route by the root layout, and resets unless the route is a replay, a day or Chronicle. The first replay after a walk sets Memories as it mounts (RM3). Map, Walks and Days share one `ChronicleMap` (no longer keyed by section, SB3), under a `SafeAreaInsetsContext` whose top is 0 (the section bar took it). **The bar names what is shown** (SB1-SB5, decision 0136): every change of the sheet's list goes through `showSheet`, which sets the section by `sectionOfSheet` (a place's walks and a day's timeline keep theirs) and clears a selected star and a Walk here card; a bar press reaches the map as a counted `BarPress`, adjusted while rendering, so a press on the selected section still resets the sheet (`sheetForSection`). The sheet's Walks keeps its zoom-out (0054); the bar's has none.
  - Since T6 (decision 0141), the bar's press reaches the map through `ChronicleMap`'s handle (`show`), not a counted `BarPress`, and opens the sheet through `openSheet`, the same path as the sheet's own controls: the bar's Walks zooms out as the sheet's does. With no map up (Memories, Media) the press only names the section, and the map that mounts opens on it with its kept view (T7 asks about a zoom-out there).
  - Since T7 (decision 0142), a `ChronicleMap` that mounts on Walks runs the walks list's zoom-out once, at its first read (`zoomOnMount`, `mountZoom`), against the camera it reported or the kept one, over the places of the walks shown (`shownPlaces`, never a walk whose Undo waits); a read that framed the places itself skips it. Days and Map mount on the kept view.
- **Views never import `db`.** A note edited in the viewer goes through `resurface.saveNote`; an edit through `captureEdits` and then `resurface.patch`.
- **E2E:** older journeys that expect the map open Chronicle with `tabTo(pg, 'chronicle')`, which now clicks Map when Memories is up; `tabTo(pg, 'chronicle', 'memories')` stays on Memories.
- **A tapped reminder opens on Memories too** (NV13): `MemoriesScreen` carries the same `onRecallOpen` handler and cold-start `takeRecallOpen` as the map's screen, since only one of the two is mounted. If you change one, change both.
- **Choices are written in a chain and Undo is per choice** (audit 2, 3, 10): each choice returns a `PrefsChange { before, after, saved }`; `undo(change)` removes only what that change added (`revertChange`), so a later rename or hide stays. A read waits for pending writes, and the newest read wins.
- **A new open and a refresh differ** (audit 4): `load()` starts a new open (a new seed, the top card first); `refresh()` reads again and keeps the open's seed, order and place. `useResurface` loads when `chronicleSection.visit()` changed (the app was elsewhere), else refreshes.
- **The Map keeps its view** (audit 8): `ChronicleMap` saves its camera and framed places into `chronicleSection.keepMap` as it unmounts and starts from them.
- **The page is strictly by time** (MC13; book order, oldest above, since JN1): no thread priority, ties by id (`journal/page.ts`). A thread's moments are reached from the rail, in the contents' Threads.
- **A moment arrives whole** (MC1-MC5, MC17): `context.ts` writes the line (street within 40 m, town, time of day, walked minutes, "just after" by kind). The shell's `contextFor(ids)` reads it once per moment per open, for the cards that mount (`ReadsContext`); `pending` and `failed` make no place claim. A refresh drops the lines of a walk that lost a capture.
- **Hidden, the switch and Forget** (MC8-MC10): `prefs.off` empties the top card and Another only; Forget keeps the switch; every sentence is in `copy.ts` and asserted verbatim.
- **Seeding the e2e's street cache:** the app clears derived caches on a fresh database as it starts, so seed streets after it is running, not before a reload.
- **Context reads are queued and never overtaken** (memories-context audit 8, 9, 2): `contextFor` runs one read at a time; a read that a refresh overtook writes nothing; a refresh keeps only finished lines and bumps `state.version`, which a mounted card watches to ask again. A failed pause read is not cached. A delete drops its walk's lines (`dropContextOfWalks`).
- **The street is worded by how it was found** (audit 17): `streetAt` says whether the street is the snapped one (bare) or found within 40 m (`Near ...`); the longitude window is divided by cos(latitude).
- **"just after" is two walked minutes, "after" ten** (audit 20); the small hours belong to the evening before (audit 10); the foot line "From ..." never names the town (audit 3).
- **A softened word is shown as written** (audit 7): `prefs.shown` keeps the owner's form beside the stored term; Undo and Forget treat it like the lists (`revertChange`).
