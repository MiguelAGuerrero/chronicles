# Owner threads

Threads the owner makes: a moment stays whole, and a thread, its members and a directed reply are separate stored records (specs/owner-threads, decision 0104). The criteria are OT1-OT24; their tests are written before their code.

- **Truth, not cache.** `thread`, `thread_member` and `moment_link` are never derived and never carry a tile build. The Chronicle's derived threads (`lib/resurface`, 0098) are a separate environment: neither reads the other.
- **No empty thread.** A thread exists only because two kept moments were connected (OT6); one left, and it dissolves (OT15).
- **Everything that reads then writes is one chain** (`serial` in `shell.ts`): two replies at once must join one thread, and a deleted moment is found out just before the write (owner-threads audit 6, 7). A new shell method follows it.
- **Both kinds are "threads"** (decision 0105): `ThreadMark` (`made`: link, `found`: repeat) tells the owner's from the Chronicle's wherever one is listed or opened; an ink icon with a spoken name, never a colour alone. The Chronicle's rail and screen use it too (components may use both modules).
- **The picker is `filterPicker`** (core): not the moment itself, nothing waiting in Undo, words or place or date, a page at a time (audit 3, 4).
- **A kept Chronicle thread has no name** (audit 13): its own name is only the name field's placeholder.
- **Three records, one writer.** `db.applyThreads(change)` is the only write; it dissolves a thread left with fewer than two members in the same transaction, in every store (the fake, IndexedDB, SQLite; `dbContract.ts` holds them to it). `deleteMoments` and `deleteWalk` cascade by hand (no foreign keys). A new write path that skips `thinThreads` can leave a thread of one.
- **Undo is memory.** `deleteThread` and `removeFromThread` schedule a job (`{ kind, ids, spec }`, the delete as data, stored by `deletion/runner.ts`) in the app's `deletions` window, and every thread write first stores what waits (`flush`); a screen shows `visibleThreads(data, useHidden())`, so a thread under two visible moments, or a hidden one, is gone at once and Undo costs nothing.
- **A reply is written after its moment.** `useCaptures` takes `replyTo` and calls `threads.reply` once `db.addMoment` resolved; both must be kept. A reply from a viewer outside a walk is a loose moment placed by one fix; a reply made from a viewer opened during a walk (`walkId`) belongs to that walk.
- **A reply made mid-walk is in its thread at once, and Memories shows it there** (fix thread-in-walk): the feed leaves out a walk still open (RM17), so `resurface.store`'s `inOpenWalk` holds those moments for the owner's threads only (`MemoriesScreen`'s `inThreadById`); Lately, the resurfaced card and the Chronicle's threads never read it. A thread screen or row that reads `byId` alone shows a thread of one.
- **The viewer never says a name** except for the thread it was opened from (`thread`); the resurfaced card, Lately, notifications and shares never carry a thread's name or a title made from it. The viewer speaks of a thread only in its info line ("In a thread") and its More menu (Open its thread); nothing sits over the moment (OT27, decision 0114).
- **A moment's responses sheet** (response thread, decision 0131): `responsesOf` (core) follows `moment_link` only, from the visible links, so a response in Undo takes those that answered only it; `CaptureViewer` keeps `sheet { root, depth, size }` and shows it only while the stack is at that depth, so a response opened from it (`openedStack`) hides it and Back ("Back to the responses") returns to it. It is a `SheetFrame` inside the viewer's Modal, above the docked toolbar, whose Respond is its bottom bar (Edit hides while it is up); `ownKeys={false}`, since the Modal's `onRequestClose` lowers it first (one Escape once read twice closed the viewer too). The pool behind it reads again when the links change (`useMomentPool(key, again)`) and includes a walk still open (`every`). Its words: `responsesWord` (its own five, tested apart from the thread sizes), `entryLine` (never a blank place or walk). E2E: `E2E_ONLY=responses`.
- **Mentions are text, not records** (moment references, decision 0125): a note's body holds `@[id|words]` (`lib/refs.ts`, core and neutral, so resurface reads it too). Readers with no lookup say the words (`plainNote`); the viewer draws `NoteText` cards from `useMomentPool` (in `components/threads`, since it reads resurface's views and `lib/threads` may not); "Mentioned in a note" is read from notes when shown (`mentioning`), never stored or counted. A new place that shows a note's text uses `plainNote`.
- **Keywords are references too** (reference keywords, decision 0141, RK1-RK20): `@{here|near|there|time|word|value|payload}` in the same body (`lib/refs.ts`: `keywordToken`, `keywordPlain`, `replaceKeywordValue`, `insertKeyword`). `lib/refKeywords.ts` (core) holds the entries, `REF_RULES`, the time words (`resolveTime` takes the timezone offset as an argument: core reads none) and `nearbyPlaces`; `lib/refPlaces` (shell, `db.placesIn` and `getSegmentsIn` only, a read that gives up after 2.5 s) and `lib/refValues` (shell, meta `ref_values`) are built on the ports. The menu (`RefPalette`, beside the field and filtering as the owner types after the @: `menuQuery`, `matchEntries`, `moveHighlight`; the web's arrows and Enter go through its handle) and the resolution picker (`RefResolve`, with a map pick) are views inside the editor's or the viewer's Modal. A keyword is a pill (`NoteText`); the field shows plain words (decision 0141: a richer field is later, so keep new kinds as entries and tokens, not editor code). **Never ask for a fix** (RK15): the editor is handed the moment's saved fix, or for a new reply `getLastFix()`; a saved note takes no stamp. A place keyword is corrected from the viewer (`onKeyword`, `saveNoteBody`); a time is not. A pointer to a deleted moment reads its word (`NoteText` inline); the keyword's setting `useRefValues` must reach every component that calls `plainNote`.
- **A note in the owner's thread is read in place** (fix thread-log, the owner's call, Nov 11): `OwnThreadScreen` draws a one-piece note as `NoteBubble` (its whole text through `NoteText`, its time, its walk, More), never a card that opens it alone full screen; a tap on the words opens nothing, and More's "Open the moment" is the way to Respond and Edit. A photo, video, memo or stacked moment keeps its `MomentCard`. The Chronicle's `ThreadScreen` is unchanged. Only a note that mentions a moment draws `NoteText` (which reads the whole moment pool per mount); a plain one is a `Text`, said as one item (`momentSpoken`), so a long thread of notes reads nothing more (audit 1, 2). The journeys find a note there as `note-bubble`, not `moment-card`.
- **No digit but a date** leaves `threads/lines.ts`; a test checks the size words are the four-phrase set.
- **Independence** is by import: `lib/threads` imports nothing from `lib/resurface` and `lib/resurface` nothing from `lib/threads` (`threads/arch.test.ts`). Components may use both; Keep as my thread copies, one way.
- **"in a thread" opens a thread** through `threads.requestOpen` / `takeOpen` and the store, then `router.navigate('/')` (Chronicle, since 0112); `MemoriesScreen` takes the request on focus and on any later change.
- **E2E:** `E2E_ONLY=threads` (seeds IndexedDB, checks the stores directly).
