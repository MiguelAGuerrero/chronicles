# The journal: the page at rest

## The page at rest, the empty page as the pen, the day's stack (JN31-JN33, Nov 13, the owner's calls)

- **At rest the sheet is at its end, and its end is made to look like a top.** The rows of the peek and the empty page (`empty-page`) share one box at least as tall as the room (`restBox`, `lib/journal/rest.ts`): the empty page grows to fill what the peek leaves, never under `blankMin`, so the platform lays out `blankHeight` of the peek in the same pass as the peek (fix entry-jitter 2; it used to be computed from the rows' measured heights, a render late) (`peekStart`: from the entry the page opened on, `anchorOf`, to the end), so the end of the sheet shows the day's date, that entry under it and nothing older. Never go back to `scrollTo` a computed offset: the empty page's height is the one thing that decides it. A peek taller than two thirds of the room shows its end; the empty page is never under `BLANK_MIN`.
- **Dates are sticky** (`stickyHeaderIndices`), on their own ground (`theme.bg`), so the date is above the last entry at rest and entries pass under it reading back.
- **The empty page is the pen.** The field is its first line and the rest is one Pressable (`line-write`, "Write an entry") whose press calls `OpenLine`'s handle (`write()`: focus inside the tap, so a phone raises its keyboard). `OpenLine` is a `forwardRef` for this: a function handed through the render prop is a ref read in render to the compiler's lint.
- **The day's entries stack; nothing rearranges** (JN33). The first build of JN32 slid the last entry away so the new one had a blank screen, and Done brought the page back with it on top: the owner's call was a stack that slides up "almost like a typewriter", so that writing never looks like words only. The anchor is the entry the page opened on (state in `JournalPage`, replaced only when that entry is gone); the rule takes no `writing` flag. Once the stack fills two thirds of the room the empty page stays a third, with the line at its top, and the sheet's end is kept in view as it grows. The keyboard changes the sheet's height in several steps while the bars fold, so `JournalPage` holds `pinned` for `SETTLE_MS` and scrolls to the end once more after it; a scroll event in between must not unpin.
- **The sheet is not seen until it can rest** (fix entry-jitter, the owner's phone, Nov 14: an entry jittered up and down on launch). The empty page's height comes from the peek's measured rows (`peekMeasured`, `lib/journal/rest`); taken to its end before they reported, the sheet was too long by the whole peek, put the entry above its resting place and slid it down as the heights arrived. `JournalPage` stays at opacity 0 and does not scroll until the entries are read (`loaded`) and the peek and its held date are measured, then opens once and goes to the end once; `sheetOpens` also wants the read answered (failed counts, so JN9's page opens at once): a slow read never shows today's date alone. A row that never reports (a reused view on the web) holds it `OPEN_WAIT_MS` after the read answered, a read that never answers `OPEN_HARD_MS` (audit 1-3). The journey samples every launch frame's last-entry position and wants one. Later height changes (a photo arriving) still move the end by a frame: not hidden.
- **At rest, the end is taken before the frame is painted** (fix entry-jitter 2, the owner's phone, Nov 14: a voice note jittered until its place was read). Each entry's place is read after the page opens, and a dateline that wraps grows its entry. iOS Safari keeps no scroll anchor, so an entry above that grew pushed the one in view down until `onContentSizeChange` brought the end back, a frame later. On the web a `useLayoutEffect` with no deps takes a pinned page to its end on every commit when `snapsToEnd` says so: never while the field has the keyboard (a keystroke would pull a finger reading back) or while the keyboard's slide is pending or under way (`moving`, `sliding`; JN33), and whether or not older entries were asked for (audit 1-3); the open scroll is a layout effect too (a `useEffect` painted the sheet's top once). A date inside the peek's box is held with react-native-web's own sticky style on the web and not held on native. The journey turns Chromium's anchoring off (`overflow-anchor: none`) to be Safari, seeds a street that wraps the datelines, and samples every frame.
- **A pill above the bar (room left, the edit offer, Undo) can cover the middle of the empty page.** It takes its own taps; the journeys tap the page's first lines (`position: { x: 40, y: 16 }`). A centre click on `line-write` hit the room-left pill and typed nowhere.

## The row under the line (JN34-JN40, decision 0175, Nov 14, the owner's mock-up review)

- **The controls are in the row or the bar, never both** (`lib/journal/bar.ts`, `controlsAt`): the bar (`open-line`) while typing, before the sheet opens (`JournalPage`'s `onOpen`), after a failed save, with the row out of view (`onAway`, now "the row is not wholly above the foot's padding"), or for a voice note begun in it (`recordingIn`: Stop never jumps when the keyboard goes); else the row (`line-row`) under the field, with a "+" (`line-plus`) in the margin. The row reuses the bar's test ids (`band-photo`, `band-voice`, `line-done`): only one is ever drawn.
- **The sheet keeps the bar's room whether or not it is drawn** (audit 43): `onHeight` reports `max(barH, aboveH)` at rest, so the sheet's length never changes as the bar comes and goes and a reader scrolled back is not moved. Report 0 at rest and the browser clamps the scroll.
- **Messages pad the sheet at rest** (JN40): the delete's `UndoToast` is in the line's column (`above`), not placed by `bottom: lineH`, so its height is in `aboveH` and a page at its end rises clear of it. Placed outside the column, it covered the row.
- **The "+" is the field to a finger**, hidden from a screen reader (audit 44); the row comes right after the field in reading order. A test of that order reads the document, never Tab from the field: focusing it raises the bar and takes the row away.
- **The glyphs line up with the line's first letter**: the row is pulled 10 px left (a 24 pt glyph in a 44 pt target). Never `flex: 0` in the row (react-native-web: it collapses), `flexGrow: 0, flexShrink: 0`.
- **"bar" is a presence tag**: a string `'bar'` anywhere in `src` fails `presence-readers audit 1`; `controlsAt` answers `'foot' | 'line'`.
