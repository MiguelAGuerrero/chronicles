# Presence and kinds

## Presence: what a place is (PR1-PR8, decision 0140)

- **One classification.** `presence/presence.ts` (core) is the only table from OpenMapTiles poi classes, Overture's taxonomy, the places archive's classes (`classesOfArchive`, PS1) and landcover (`classesOfLandcover`, PS4) to what a place is: `classesOfOsm(cls, sub)` (a class's own rules, then a subclass the same under any class, then the class's default) and `classesOfOverture(hierarchy)` give classes from one vocabulary (`CLASSES`: each a density weight and, where it has one, a category and kind). Density reads `storefrontWeight` (the greatest of its storefront classes, below), marks and bars `markClassOf` (the first with a category). A feature may carry several classes.
- **Curation is apart.** `discovery/curation.ts` decides what is a mark over classes (`NEVER_MARK`, `BY_NAME`, `MARK_CATEGORIES`); presence says what a place is, never whether it is worth finding. Presence records two facts curation judges (PS3, PS6, PS10): `recorded` (the archive holds it) and `historic_name` (`presence/notable.ts`, the one word list and rule); a `BY_NAME` class is a mark with either. A third, `by_type`, marks an archive place held for its type alone (contract v6's `museum_by_type`, v7's `natural_monument`, v8's `suspension_bridge`): never `recorded`, and `named` never makes it `historic` (more-attractions audit 2). Both weigh nothing; nothing sorts by them (0103: a gate, never a measure).
- **Change a class or a table, bump `PRESENCE_CODE`.** It is part of `ATLAS_CODE` (`a14.p4`) and `LANDING_VERSION`, so atlas and landing rows are read again; when the golden tables show nothing derived changed, carry the last code's rows: `PRESENCE_CARRIED` beside the code, read by the atlas's `CARRIED` and the landing's `LANDING_CARRIED` (PS9: `a12.p1` under `a12.p2`, by `ATLAS_OWN`); only ever the code just before, emptied when a bar, mark or weight changes (presence-readers audit 2). Rows and near reads judge a code alike (`codeCurrent`, audit 4). Cached places keep their read's category (nothing reads them); checklists take a new class at the next tile build.
- **Every reader is on presence** (specs/presence-readers): the archive, bars, notable names, greens and the planner. A place carries `classes` on a fresh read, never stored. It also holds `unnamedLabel` (D4) and `isTransitStop` (L7), and lists every tag it reads (`SOURCE_TAGS`), which the guard (presence-readers audit 1) holds no module outside presence spells, the kind pipeline's among them. Lookups read own entries (`own`, audit 5: `constructor` threw). Traffic, if ever, is a signal of its own (0140).
- **Storefronts** (specs/storefronts SF10, decision 0180): a class with `storefront: true` is a place you walk into; density (`activity/weights.ts`, so the Storefronts layer, What's here?, a town's pockets, the atlas's strips and the leaning) reads `storefrontWeight`, never `densityWeight`, which only a complex's shopping word reads. Parks and green, monuments and memorials, public art, viewpoints, curiosities, listed structures and cemeteries are not storefronts. Changing the flag is a weight change: bump `PRESENCE_CODE` with nothing carried (p3).
- **Overture reaches storefronts through its roots** (tend presence 2, SF10; the owner, Nov 19 and 20, decision 0238): `cultural_and_historic` is a storefront `heritage` and `sports_and_recreation` a storefront `sport`, so a deeper Overture name of a kind that weighs nothing (a memorial site and its cemeteries, a park, a trail) needs its own `OVERTURE_DEEPER` row, of a class that is no storefront; a park or a trail takes `open_space`, no category, so nothing reading Overture's classes could make a mark (0109; only `activity/weights.ts` and `complexes/rule.ts` read them). A historic site is a place you can enter: `heritage`, a storefront with no mark ("Enterable counts both", tend presence audit 2). The fixtures (`landing/phillipsburg.json`, `complexes/{whitehall,harrisburg}.json`) are the only taxonomy the repo holds.
- **Enterable counts both** (tend presence audit 2; the owner, Nov 20: "Manners or any other monumental structures that you can enter and browse should be counted both as storefront and points of interest"): a castle-class house from the map (a castle, a manor, a fort; never `castle/ruins`, a Ruin) is `monument` and, beside it, `heritage` (`enterable` in `classesOfOsm`): marks and bars read the monument first, storefronts the heritage. A plain monument, a statue, a park, a cemetery and a memorial still weigh nothing.
- **Readers' golden:** `__tests__/presence/readers.golden.json` (captured on main before the slice's code) holds the archive's classes, the poi pairs under three names, the landcover greens and the atlas's marks with their kinds over presenceSample and presenceTowns, also fed outlines (PS8, audit 8). A deliberate change re-captures it, listed in a spec.
- **Golden table:** `__tests__/presence/golden.json` holds what the two old tables answered for every realistic pair; PR8 holds every pair outside PR5's list to it. A deliberate reclassification adds the pair to the test's `CHANGED` with its new answer and to the spec's PR5 table.

## Kinds: bars, community spaces, the kind pipeline, community parks

- **A place may light two bars** (PK1-PK19, decision 0127): `barsOf` (`discovery/bars.ts`, core) is the one rule for both the bars and the planner: a place's category, plus History only from presence's class `historic` (PS11): a museum or gallery the archive records, or whose name is notable once a neighbourhood's “Old Town” and “old and new” are read past (`named`, audit 5; marks read the name whole, PS3). A place without `classes` lights its category alone. A plain museum stays in Culture alone, so History and Culture stay apart (PK5, held by `presenceSample.json`, recorded by `scripts/fixture-presence.mjs`). Re-run PK5 before a second bar for a whole kind. History for the archive's `museum` rests on contract v2 (a museum enters only by its listing): the pinned contract's README says so (PK19).
- **Community spaces** (`community`, in Culture): `town_hall/community_centre`, `library/library` and `community_centre` by a notable name only (presence class `community`, in curation's `BY_NAME`), `art_gallery/arts_centre` and `arts_centre` as any name (class `arts_centre`). `parseHidden(raw, known)`: a choice saved before the kind existed (meta `explore_kinds_known` lacks it, written by `saveSettings`) keeps it off when Museums and galleries is off (PK18); every reader of `explore_hidden` goes through `parseHidden` (the scenic read too).
- **The kind pipeline** (PC18-PC23, decisions 0143, 0144): a registry of pure refinements splits a kind into finer subsets.
  adapter (`tilePois`) -> presence classes -> curation (marks) -> merge of sources (`mergePlaces`) -> **refinement**
  (`discovery/refine.ts` the engine, `refinements.ts` the registry) -> the reader's switches. A `Refinement` is `{ id, parent,
  child, judge(places, ctx) => (place) => boolean }`; `refine(places, ctx, refinements)` returns new places, never mutates, applies in order
  (a child may be a later parent). **A new subset is a new entry in `REFINEMENTS`** plus its child in `KINDS` (after its parent) and
  `Kind`; its words go in a JSON file. The adapter and the merge judge nothing about parks. The reads that feed checklists
  and the atlas call `refineRead(merged, { landcover, archive })` after the merge (`landing/read.ts`, `ringOf`); a read with no
  `landcover` judges by name and what is near the point. Never test a kind against the switches with `hidden.has(kind)`: use
  `isKindHidden(hidden, kind)` (a parent off hides its children, `kindChain`), and `kindSwitch` for a row (off and locked
  while a parent is off).
- **Community parks** (PC1-PC17, decisions 0143, 0163): the first refinement, `discovery/parkKind.ts` (`communityParks`). A park whose
  name holds a word of `communityWords.json` (playground, tot lot, courts), or whose `landcover` outline is under 2 ha (and over a 500 m2 sliver), is the kind `community_parks`
  when nothing notable is in or near it: a mark inside the `landcover` outline (`discovery/green.ts`, `outlinesOf`; within 60 m
  of the point with none), a notable name (presence's `notableName`, read past a neighbourhood's "Old Town/City/Quarter/Village" or "old and new" only just before a community word) or a word of `parkNotable.json` ("heritage" in neither), an outline of 5 ha or more, or an archive place
  within 150 m (the refinement's context). **An outline under 2 ha, not cut at a tile edge, demotes only with nothing notable (0163).** The kind is of the parks category (same bar, colour, find,
  mark); a switch hides only it. **Never name an unfound place for its kind** on the map or the Explore tab: bars and
  waiting kinds are categories, so "Community parks" shows in "What I'm into", on a found place (`foundLine`), and since
  decision 0254 on a named unfound place's card, its kind line and its kinds sheet ("Parks and gardens · Community parks",
  `cardKinds`; a "?" card names it only in its sheet; poi-hide-from-card audit 13). The planner and scenic routes do not
  read "What I'm into" and count both as parks. It is curation (0140), so a change to the words bumps `ATLAS_CODE`'s own
  part, never `PRESENCE_CODE`.
  Its two word files are the refinement's curation, never a second notable rule (PS3, 0146); outlines read presence (PS4).
- **The neighbourhood read-past is one list, read after `normalizeWords`** (decision 0172, tend presence 1): `NEIGHBOURHOOD` in `presence/notable.ts` ("old town/city/quarter/village", a possessive "towns" too, and "old and new", over the normalized name), read by `notableBeyondNeighbourhood` and by `parkKind.ts`'s `pastNeighbourhood`, so "Old-Town", "Old_Town", "Old.Town" and "Old&New" read as "Old Town" and "Old & New" do. The owner made a change to it a `PRESENCE_CODE` bump with nothing carried (p5), whichever reader it moves; a regex on the raw name misses every spelling `normalizeWords` folds.
- **The read-past's phrases, as built** (tend presence audit 1, the owner's answer of Nov 16; this line supersedes the list in the line above, whose `NEIGHBOURHOOD` is no longer exported): `readPastNeighbourhood(name, before?)` in `presence/notable.ts` is the one read, over the name after `normalizeWords`, building its own regex on each call (never a shared `/g` object). Its phrases: "old town", "old city", "old village", their plurals and possessives ("towns", "cities", "citys", "villages"), "old quarter" but never "old quarters" (lodgings name a building; a possessive "Old Quarter's" cannot be told from "Old Quarters" after `normalizeWords`, so it keeps its museum), "old and new", and the archaic and joined "old towne", "olde town", "olde towne", "oldtown". A museum reads every phrase past; community parks passes `before` to read one past only just before a community word. The phrases are presence's (`PRESENCE_CODE`); only that condition is the refinement's (`ATLAS_OWN`).
