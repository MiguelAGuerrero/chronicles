# Expectations: presence

What a person would reasonably expect of presence (decision 0236): drafted by `/tend`'s lens 0, ratified by the
owner. Changed only through `npm run expectations`; a line's words, status and answer are the owner's.

- E-presence-1 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using presence, would reasonably expect a "?" card to name no kind until the place is found, and the find then to be kept under the kind whose row in "What I'm into" hides it.
  Check: pending "a '?' card names no kind until the place is found; the find is kept under the kind whose row hides it"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; not yet recorded (how adjacent apps name a place still to find, and keep a find under a kind)
- E-presence-2 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using presence, would reasonably expect a street listed under Storefronts to be lined with places they could walk into (a shop, a café, a museum), never lit by a park, a monument or memorial, or a cemetery, whichever map holds it.
  Check: test:"tend presence 2: an Overture historic site, park, trail, memorial site or cemetery weighs nothing toward storefronts, as the map's do"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "Say what an area holds: findable places apart from the places you go into" (Google Maps, Walk Score as a counter-example for its score; unverified)
- E-presence-3 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using presence, would reasonably expect a kind they switch off in "What I'm into" to stop lighting its area's card, and to light it again when they switch it back on.
  Check: test:"SG12: a Sights kind switched off leaves its bar; back on restores it; Local life has no switch"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "Choose what you are shown: a long list of switches" (iOS Settings, Android notification categories; verified); decision 0115 ("kinds filter what you see, never what exists")
- E-presence-4 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using presence, would reasonably expect a place that both the map and the places archive hold to show once, as one place, never as two marks side by side.
  Check: test:"remarkable-and-public audit 5: a listed cemetery 120 m from the tiles' same-named one is one place to find"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; not yet recorded (how adjacent apps show one place two sources hold)
- E-presence-5 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using presence, would reasonably expect a place with no name to still say what it is, in words ("A fountain", "A war memorial"), never left blank or shown as a code.
  Check: test:"XS13: an unnamed place is named by its type, said once when found"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; not yet recorded (how adjacent apps label an unnamed point of interest)
