# Expectations: walk-screen

What a person would reasonably expect of walk-screen (decision 0236): drafted by `/tend`'s lens 0, ratified by the
owner. Changed only through `npm run expectations`; a line's words, status and answer are the owner's.

- E-walk-screen-1 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using the walk screen, would reasonably expect the way they are following to stay readable at a glance on a small phone: how far and where to, whole, and the time left only on a way they asked for themselves.
  Check: walk:"tend walk-screen T42: the navigation bar is two rows beside Minimize, the line whole on up to two lines over Open in Maps and End in words, 44 pt, at 390 and 320 ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "Guide a drive" (Apple Maps, Organic Maps: guidance on the device; the bar's layout not yet recorded)
  Serves: none; it reads as what map apps have (a legible guidance bar), not what raises what this person notices
- E-walk-screen-2 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using the walk screen, would reasonably expect a photo taken while being guided somewhere to be kept on the walk, the walk going on recording under the guidance.
  Check: walk:"N12: while navigating, the walk records, a photo is kept, Pause holds the bar's distance, and the fog draws ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; VISION Always, "A moment carries its context"; adjacent-apps "Record a walk: start, pause, stop" (AllTrails: photos taken during the recording are placed on the line; unverified)
  Serves: Honour; upholds "A moment carries its context": a photo taken under guidance keeps its walk
- E-walk-screen-3 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using the walk screen, would reasonably expect nothing to be recorded while the walk is paused.
  Check: walk:"nothing is recorded while paused ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; VISION Always, "Moments and the trace are truth"; VISION Never, "All-day tracking"; adjacent-apps "Record a walk: start, pause, stop" (AllTrails, Komoot: pause and resume; unverified)
  Serves: Honour; upholds "Moments and the trace are truth": the trace holds only the walking they chose to record
- E-walk-screen-4 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using the walk screen, would reasonably expect ending a walk to take a deliberate hold, not a stray tap, and to bring the walk straight back as its replay.
  Check: walk:"holding Stop opens the replay"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; VISION "What it is"; decisions 0008, 0048, 0220 (the hold, "protection against accidents"); adjacent-apps "Record a walk: start, pause, stop" (AllTrails: Finish to save; unverified; a hold not yet recorded)
  Serves: Honour, Return; Honour: a stray tap never ends the walk; Return: the replay comes straight after
- E-walk-screen-5 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using the walk screen, would reasonably expect a photo, a voice memo or a note to be one tap from the screen, ready or walking, each kept where and when it was taken.
  Check: pending "on the ready screen and walking, the camera, the mic and the note each one tap from the stack at the edge, each kept with its place and time"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; VISION Always, "Capture is one tap away"; adjacent-apps "Capture in one tap" (Day One, Apple Journal)
