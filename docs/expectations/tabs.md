# Expectations: tabs

What a person would reasonably expect of tabs (decision 0236): drafted by `/tend`'s lens 0, ratified by the
owner. Changed only through `npm run expectations`; a line's words, status and answer are the owner's.

- E-tabs-1 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using tabs, would reasonably expect the app, opened with no walk under way, to land on Chronicle's Memories, where their moments are, not on a map.
  Check: capturehome:"HC1: the app opens on Memories at /, one tab bar, no live map ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; VISION "What it is" ("Chronicle first", decision 0209) and decision 0112; adjacent-apps "Capture in one tap" (Day One and Apple Journal open on the journal itself; verified)
- E-tabs-2 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using tabs, would reasonably expect the app, opened while a walk is still recording, to bring that walk straight up, with no glimpse of another screen first.

  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "Record a walk: start, pause, stop" (AllTrails, Komoot; unverified); not yet recorded (how they reopen on a recording under way)
- E-tabs-3 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using tabs, would reasonably expect a walk they leave for another tab to go on recording, with a bar on that tab saying so.
