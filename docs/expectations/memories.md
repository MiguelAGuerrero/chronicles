# Expectations: memories

What a person would reasonably expect of memories (decision 0236): drafted by `/tend`'s lens 0, ratified by the
owner. Changed only through `npm run expectations`; a line's words, status and answer are the owner's.

- E-memories-1 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using memories, would reasonably expect what they kept at a place on an earlier walk to show on the walk's map when they pass that place again.
  Check: memories:"a later walk shows the earlier walk's captures nearby as memory pins, minus the one being reminded (MR1, MR6, MR7: "
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "A feed, resurfacing, 'a year ago today'" (Apple Photos' memories, which include places; Day One's On This Day, by date)
  Serves: Return
- E-memories-2 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using memories, would reasonably expect a tap on such a memory to say what it was, when and on which walk, and to open the moments kept at that spot.
  Check: memories:"a tap previews the memory with its date and walk (MR4: "
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "Replay a walk; moments on a map" (Day One's map: a pin opens its entries) and "View one moment full screen" (Apple Photos, Google Photos); Apple Photos' places not yet recorded
  Serves: Return; upholds "A moment carries its context": the preview names its date and its walk
- E-memories-3 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using memories, would reasonably expect a memory to read plainly as kept on an earlier walk, apart from what this walk keeps.
  Check: memories:"memory pins are faint, their clock solid, and named as memories (MR3, MR10: opacity "
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "Where you have been: visits and history" (Google Maps Timeline, Apple Maps Visited Places; neither draws a past moment as a pin)
  Serves: Return
- E-memories-4 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using memories, would reasonably expect a memory they did not ask to be reminded of never to buzz, notify or call for them while they walk: it is there when they look.
  Check: memories:"opening a memory never brings a card or a buzz of its own"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "A feed, resurfacing, 'a year ago today'" (Day One keeps On This Day's notifications a separate setting)
  Serves: Return; upholds "Restraint in motion, colour and copy": a memory waits to be looked at
