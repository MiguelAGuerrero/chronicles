# Expectations: gallery

What a person would reasonably expect of gallery (decision 0236): drafted by `/tend`'s lens 0, ratified by the
owner. Changed only through `npm run expectations`; a line's words, status and answer are the owner's.

- E-gallery-1 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using gallery, would reasonably expect every photo, video and voice memo they kept, on a walk or between walks, the dull ones too, in one place they can scroll through quickly, none left out or picked for them.
  Check: test:"MG3: one tile per photo, video and memo piece, newest first, no notes, no open walk"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "Group and search" (Day One's Media view, filtered by kind); Apple Photos' library grid not yet recorded
  Serves: Return; upholds "The lows stay in": every piece kept has its tile, none picked
- E-gallery-2 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using gallery, would reasonably expect each day of pictures to say when and where it was kept, by its walk or its street, in words, never a count of pictures, so the grid is never only thumbnails.
  Check: gallery:"MG6: each day says where ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "A feed, resurfacing, 'a year ago today'" (Day One's List under date headings); Apple Photos' day headers not yet recorded
  Serves: Return; upholds "A moment carries its context": the day's header names its walk or its street, in words
- E-gallery-3 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using gallery, would reasonably expect a tap on a picture to open its moment whole, with its words, and where, when and on which walk it was kept, readable without opening anything more.
  Check: gallery:"tend gallery 2: the viewer's info line shows the moment's place and walk uncut, on its own row above the step buttons ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "View one moment full screen" (Apple Photos, Google Photos)
  Serves: Return; upholds "A moment carries its context": the viewer's info line is whole
- E-gallery-4 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using gallery, would reasonably expect to go back to an earlier month or year and see where they wandered then, named in words, and open its days in a tap.
  Check: test:"MG19: months and years with their cover and place in words"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; Apple Photos' Years, Months and Days not yet recorded (specs/media-gallery, Decisions)
  Serves: Return
