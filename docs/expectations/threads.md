# Expectations: threads

What a person would reasonably expect of threads (decision 0236): drafted by `/tend`'s lens 0, ratified by the
owner. Changed only through `npm run expectations`; a line's words, status and answer are the owner's.

- E-threads-1 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using threads, would reasonably expect to put a moment they have just kept into a thread they made in a tap or two, without leaving the walk.
  Check: capthread:"TK10: three murals, one thread, made from the pill without leaving the walk ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "File a moment into a group as it is kept" (Lightroom Classic's target collection; Google Keep's labels; Pinterest's board, asked there at Save, here only after the moment is kept, never before it)
  Serves: Return; the owner's threads are gathered where the moments are made
- E-threads-2 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using threads, would reasonably expect every moment offered to be joined to show what it is, its picture or its words, with its place, time and walk, never a bare picture.
  Check: capthread:"TK6, TK7: mid-walk the first mural is offered, newest first, with its picture beside its words and line ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "File a moment into a group as it is kept" (Google Keep's labels list items by their words)
  Serves: Return; upholds "A moment carries its context": a picker row is a picture with its words and its line
- E-threads-3 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using threads, would reasonably expect the thread they last added to to be offered first when they keep the next moment of a series.
  Check: capthread:"TK4: the thread last added to is first, under "Last added to" ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; adjacent-apps "File a moment into a group as it is kept" (Lightroom Classic's target collection, one key to a remembered target; Pinterest's last board first)
  Serves: Return; a series is gathered as it is made, with no scrolling back
