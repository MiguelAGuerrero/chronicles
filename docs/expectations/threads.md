# Expectations: threads

What a person would reasonably expect of threads (decision 0236): drafted by `/tend`'s lens 0, ratified by the
owner. Changed only through `npm run expectations`; a line's words, status and answer are the owner's.

- E-threads-1 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using threads, would reasonably expect to put a moment they have just kept with others like it in a tap or two, without leaving the walk or scrolling back through their timeline.
  Check: capthread:"CT4, CT10: the third mural joined Murals in two taps ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; the owner's /investigate, Nov 21 (murals and graffiti grouped on the walk); adjacent-apps "File a moment into a group as it is kept" (Lightroom Classic's target collection, one key; unverified. Google Keep's label menu; verified)
  Serves: Return; a thread brings a moment back beside others like it
- E-threads-2 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using threads, would reasonably expect a thread to hold only the moments they connected themselves, named only if they name it.
  Check: capthread:"CT8, CT10: a thread of the two murals, named Murals as typed ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; VISION Never, "Writing the owner's words for them"; decision 0104 (threads the owner makes); adjacent-apps "Reply to a moment; a thread across days" (Slack's threads, made by a reply; verified)
  Serves: Return; upholds Never "Writing the owner's words for them": nothing names a thread for them
- E-threads-3 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using threads, would reasonably expect every moment offered for a thread to show when it was kept and which walk it came from, a photo with its picture, never a bare picture or a bare word.
  Check: capthread:"CT6: the walk being made is offered, newest first, its photo first and from its walk ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; VISION Always, "A moment carries its context"; adjacent-apps "Group and search" (Day One's Media view by kind; verified)
  Serves: Return; upholds Always "A moment carries its context"
