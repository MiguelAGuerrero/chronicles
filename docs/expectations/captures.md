# Expectations: captures

What a person would reasonably expect of captures (decision 0236): drafted by `/tend`'s lens 0, ratified by the
owner. Changed only through `npm run expectations`; a line's words, status and answer are the owner's.

- E-captures-1 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using captures, would reasonably expect a photo or video they take to go straight from the camera to its editor, never back to the screen beneath first.
  Check: edit:"tend captures audit 10: on the walk a 4032 x 3024 photo covers the map from its first opaque frame, only the mount frame before it, within 250 ms of the editor's first frame, and its box never moves ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; not yet recorded (adjacent-apps records no camera-to-editor hand-off; the owner's own report, T62, Nov 24)
  Serves: Honour; the moment arrives whole, from the shutter to its editor
- E-captures-2 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using captures, would reasonably expect a photo or video to be kept as taken the moment it is taken, so closing its editor before doing anything never loses it.
  Check: edit:"tend captures audit 7: on the walk ✕ closes the held shot before its keep settles; the photo is kept and no editor opens for it ("
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; not yet recorded (decision 0240's "Close with no change keeps it as taken", extended to the held shot by the owner, decision 0263)
  Serves: Honour
- E-captures-3 [proposed] A person who goes out to notice, using captures, would reasonably expect a capture that could not be saved to be held and offered again with Retry, never silently lost.
  Check: unsaved:"UN2: the bar's held photo shows on Memories, with Retry"
  Grounds: VISION "Who it is for"; not yet recorded (unsaved captures, decision 0196)
  Serves: Honour
