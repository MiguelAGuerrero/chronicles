# 0166. The journal's Type slot is a reaction sign, not a word

Date: 2026-11-13 · Status: accepted · Area: journal, captures, vision · Amends: decision 0161 (note types), the gate of Nov 13 in `specs/note-types/spec.md`

## Context
The vision gate of Nov 13 (note types at capture) argued that the word stays "Type" and never "reaction": the word
for likes on someone else's post, the reading decision 0161 worked to avoid. The slot shipped as a muted tag the
owner could not find (decision 0165), and was then given the word "Type". The owner, the same evening: "I would
instead just make it an emoji indicator for like a reaction as an icon so that way it doesn't explicitly say type so
it's kind of like a reaction in that type of sense."

## Decision
The owner's call, Nov 13, over the gate's argument (made once):
- **The slot is a sign**: unmarked, a face with a small plus, the sign people already know for adding a reaction;
  marked, the type's own glyph. In ink, at a target's size, with no word beside it.
- **It may read as a reaction.** It is still the owner's own mark on their own note: never counted, summed, filtered,
  ranked, searched, shown to anyone or applied to a place (0161 stands in full).

## Consequences
- The spoken name is still "Type, <name>", and More and the page's tool still say "Type"; whether those become
  "Reaction", and whether the marks become colour emoji in place of ink glyphs, are open with the owner.
- The VISION.md edit 0161 proposed (a mark on your own note, shown to no one) is still the owner's to make; this
  decision leans on it harder.
