# 0105. Threads: one word for both kinds, told apart by a mark

Date: 2026-11-09 · Status: accepted · Area: chronicle, vision

## Context
Owner-made threads (decision 0104) and the Chronicle's suggested threads (0098) were both "threads", and the audit of `specs/owner-threads` (finding 18, the steward's S6) flagged the two headings as confusable. The build had renamed the owner's block "Threads you made", a name the owner did not choose.

## Decision
The owner's call, Nov 9:
- **Keep "thread" for both.** The owner's block is headed **Threads**, as the Chronicle's rail is; neither is renamed.
- **A mark tells them apart**, an icon (the owner offered a colour or an icon): a link for a thread the owner made, a repeat arrow for one the Chronicle suggests from the owner's own words. The icon is ink-coloured and carries a spoken name ("connected by you", "found for you"). It is not a colour on its own, for the sake of restraint (`docs/VISION.md`: "Restraint in motion, colour and copy") and of a screen reader.
- **An unnamed thread's heading may change** as its moments do (the place and date of its oldest moment, its first words beneath) and the owner may rename it whenever they like; a name they gave never changes by itself.

## Consequences
- OT12 is amended and OT25, OT26 are added to `specs/owner-threads/spec.md`.
- "Threads you made" and "A thread you made" go; so does the DEFERRED note on the two headings.
- The viewer's "In a thread" chip, which already says nothing of the kind, is unchanged.
