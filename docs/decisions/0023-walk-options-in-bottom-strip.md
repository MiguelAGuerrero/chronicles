# 0023. Walk options sit in the bottom strip, left of Pause

Date: 2026-11-01 (v1) · Status: accepted · Area: walk screen

## Context
The walk gained an Explore switch and settings in v1. The owner's call, v1: walk-level settings stay with the walk's controls.

## Decision
Walk options (the Explore switch, and a link to Settings) sit in the bottom strip, left of Pause.

## Consequences
- The options button is always `options-outline` (U11, 0051): a compass read as the Explore tab.
- Share GPS log lives in the walk's options (0027).
