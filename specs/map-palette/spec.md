# The map's palette: Earth and ink

Nov 17, 2026 · @Miguel Guerrero

The map's own colours change as one set. The walked streets and the walk's end marker move off the accent to a
blue-violet ink, the trace keeps its green, and each kind of moment gets an earth pigment. The accent, the ink and the
muted grey of the screens stay as they are. Nothing else changes: no words, no shapes, no sizes, no layers.

## Criteria

1. **MP1** The owner's task, in their words: *re-imagine the map's palette*, built as "Earth and ink". The change is the
   map's colours only: no word, shape, size, layer or control is added, moved or removed, so there is nothing new to
   find (decision 0167's finding criterion has no control to hold). A person who has walked before sees the same map
   with new colours.
2. **MP2** Each colour by its role:

   | Role | Colour | Today |
   |---|---|---|

   | The end marker, its centre and outer ring | `#5b5d9f` | `#c2410c` |
   | The trace, its rides, its faint stretches and a gap's way being drawn; the start marker's ring | `#15803d` green, unchanged | `#15803d` |
   | A photo's pin, and a moment of several pieces (a stacked moment) | `#286766` verdigris | `#0f766e` |
   | A video's pin | `#87244e` madder | `#b42318` |
   | A voice memo's pin | `#544516` raw umber | `#7b2cbf` |
   | A note's pin | `#7b7469` stone | `#a16207` |
   | A pin of several, its head (its two discs take their kinds' colours) | `#31352f` charcoal | `#3f3f46` |

   The pin of several's colour lives beside the others (a named constant with the kinds), not inside the pin's view.
3. **MP3** What stays exactly as it is: the accent, ink and muted; the route's blue `#2563eb`; the walker's navy
   `#1d3557`; the fog; the hotspot magenta `#d6268c` and its ink `#86145a`; the town borders (`BORDER`, still the
   accent's hex, out of scope); the place kinds' colours on Explore; the danger red `#b42318` of the screens (today's
   video hex, which stays the danger colour). Explore's "Walked once", its footsteps and the mini-map's walked path are
   the trace's green, so Explore does not change.
4. **MP4** Every line and marker is 3:1 or more on the map's ground (Positron's land, `#f2f3f0`): the walked streets and
   the end marker 5.37, the trace and the start ring 4.50. On beat one the lit streets are the walked colour under the
   trace's white edge (at 0.9 of the trace's 0.3) and the trace (at 0.3), which reads `#658694`, 3.50:1 on the land and
   3.04:1 on the e2e's darker ground. The test computes the blend from the opacities the maps use, not from copied
   numbers.
5. **MP5** Every pin's disc is 3:1 or more on the ground and its white glyph 4.5:1 or more on the disc: photo 6.52,
   video 8.78, voice memo 9.38, note 4.62, several 12.49. A pin of several's two discs keep their glyph at 4.5:1 too.
6. **MP6** The colour-blind floor: among the seven roles, beat one's lit street and the accent, every pair is 9.5 or
   more apart (CIEDE2000) as seen with deuteranopia and with protanopia (Machado et al. 2009, full severity, on linear
   RGB), and 15 or more in normal vision. Today's worst pair is 1.1 (the walked streets and a note, deuteranopia);
   Earth and ink's is 9.6 (a voice memo and the accent, protanopia), its worst in normal vision 17.5 (a voice memo and
   a pin of several).
7. **MP7** The accent is kept off the map: every map colour of MP2 is 20 or more from the accent (CIEDE2000; the
   closest is the note, 26.2) and none lies in the accent's family (CIELAB hue 5° to 80° with chroma over 15). On the
   replay nothing the walk lights is the accent: beat one's lit streets, the path and the card's end marker read in the
   walked ink, never `#c2410c` (decision 0153).
8. **MP8** The magenta's rule is amended, with its reason. `colors.test.ts` held every other map colour 20° or more of
   hue from the hotspot magenta (HSV). The madder video is 9.3° from it in that measure, yet it is a dark disc with a
   white glyph (lightness 32 against the magenta's 49) and never a line, fill or label, and every hue that clears 20°
   breaks MP6 (a video and a pin of several fall to 6.1 apart with protanopia at 10° further round, 3.2 at 15°). So the
   rule becomes: every map colour is 15 or more apart from the magenta (CIEDE2000, normal vision; the video is 17.5),
   hues compared round the circle. The trace's own rule (25° of hue from every other layer) is unchanged. Subject to
   the owner's answer to Open 1.
9. **MP9** The kinds' colours reach every place a moment's kind is drawn in colour, from the one set of constants:
   - the pins on the walk screen, the replay, a day's replay and Chronicle's map; a memory's pin (smaller and faded,

   No file keeps one of today's map hexes for a kind (`#0f766e`, `#7b2cbf`, `#a16207`, `#3f3f46`; `#b42318` only as the
   screens' danger colour).

    the path, the card), and the end marker on the replay and a day's replay. The walk screen, Chronicle's map,

## What was measured, and what it leaves

- The figures are from the comparison of Nov 17 (`palettes/index.md` in that run's scratchpad), measured again for this
  spec. The pictures used the e2e's fake map; the live map's figures are computed on Positron's land.
- **The note's stone.** The weak spot of the palette: a stone-grey note can read close to a disabled grey. It holds
  4.62:1 for its glyph and 4.15:1 on the ground. The UI review asks of the pictures, cold, whether a note pin reads as a
  pin you can open.
- **The end marker on beat one stays faint**, as today (1.52:1 against today's 1.54:1): it is the layer's opacity, not
  its hue. Not changed here.
- **A memory's faded pin** (55% opacity) stays under the glyph floor, as today: the note's falls from 2.27:1 to 2.13:1,
  the photo's rises from 2.39 to 2.52. MR3's fade is unchanged.
- **Beside the place kinds** (Explore's dots and "?"), the closest pair is a video pin and a shop, 8.3 apart, where
  today's closest is the walked streets and food, 3.4.
- **The walk screen's other colours.** The video pin and a found place's magenta dot are 17.5 apart (14.9 with
  protanopia); the video pin and the storefront names' ink are 5.3 apart (Open 1). The walked colour shares no map with
  the hotspots, the route or the walkability tints: the replay draws none of them.
- Not measured: dark appearance (the app has none on the map), real Positron tiles in a picture, a phone.
