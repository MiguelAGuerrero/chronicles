## How adjacent apps do it

- **Apple Voice Memos** (iOS 27): one Record button; "saved with the name New Recording or the name of your location, if Location Services is turned on". Source: https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/make-a-recording-iph4d2a39a3b/27/ios/27 . **Verified**. The model for the mic that records in place, no screen opened.
- **AllTrails**: Record, then pause and resume, Finish to save; photos taken during the recording are placed on the line. Source: from memory, unverified (support.alltrails.com unreachable from cloud sessions, Nov 17). **Unverified**.
- **Apple Journal** (iOS 27): Home Screen quick actions New Entry, Add Photos and Record Audio, each one press from outside the journal. Source: https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/write-in-your-journal-iph9824e83ce/27/ios/27 . **Verified**. The three kinds side by side, as the bar's camera, mic and pencil.

## Criteria

1. **BC1** (the owner's task: "a mic and a pencil beside the bar's camera") While a walk is open and minimized, recording or paused, the bar on every tab but Memories holds a mic and a pencil beside its camera, then Stop, each 44 pt, ink on white, with a spoken name ("Add to your walk: record a voice memo", "Add to your walk: write a note"); on Memories it holds none of the three, as today. **Finding:** someone told only "record a voice memo for this walk without opening it" finds the mic on the bar at the foot of the tab they are on, by the microphone people already know, beside the camera they already use there.
2. **BC2** (the memo records in place) The mic records on the tab: the bar's line shows the memo's time beside a red dot, Discard takes the camera's place and Stop and save (purple, as on the walk screen) the mic's, the pencil's place stays empty so Stop never moves; the memo joins the open walk, placed as a walk capture is, and the walk screen does not open. A memo under a second is not kept, said on the bar ("Too short, not kept"). A memo that fails to save is held for Retry where the bar has none (unsaved captures UN10), and the bar's words say where.
3. **BC3** (a memo is never lost to leaving) Any way the bar goes or its captures leave sight while a memo records saves it first (`memoGate` tracks the save): another tab, Memories, a screen over the tabs (a day, a replay), the reminder's See, and the bar's line, which then returns to the walk. A reload or close of the web page mid-memo is warned of by the browser, as on Navigate's ready screen. The bar's Stop keeps its spoken name, "Hold to stop the walk" (the later drawing, Said320A, over Memo390's), refuses under a memo recording with "A voice memo is recording. Save it first.", as today (decision 0048), and goes through one being saved as decision 0227 says.
4. **BC4** (the pencil) The pencil opens the note the walk screen's pencil opens (the note's page), over the tab it was tapped from; kept, the note joins the open walk; left empty, nothing is kept; a save that fails keeps the page open, "Not saved yet" (note pages), as from the walk screen.
5. **BC5** (paused) The camera, the mic and the pencil all work while the walk is paused, and what they keep joins the walk, as the walk screen's captures do.
6. **BC6** (one row) At 320 px and 390 px wide at the default text size, the bar is one row: the dot, the time, the camera, the mic, the pencil and Stop, nothing wrapped or cut; at 320 px the chevron is left out, at 390 px it shows. The line's visible words are the time alone ("12 min"; "12 min · Paused" paused); the distance stays in the line's spoken name. While a memo records the line has no chevron at any width (the drawing's Memo390 and Said320A); it shows the memo's time and its spoken name says it records and whether the walk is paused ("Recording voice memo, 0:07, walk paused. Saves it and returns to the walk").
7. **BC7** (restraint) No accent colour, count or confirmation of a routine save on the bar (matrix 20): a memo or a note kept says nothing there.
8. **BC8** (E-tabs-9) With a walk minimized, from Explore and from Chronicle's Walks, the camera, the mic and the note are each one tap from the bar, each kept in the open walk, the URL never `/walk`.
9. **BC9** (a discarded memo's Undo; approved Nov 22) Discard on the bar sets the memo aside with the walk screen's pill, "Voice memo discarded" with Undo, on the page's ground just above the bar; Undo keeps the memo in the walk. The pill goes when Undo is used or after the walk screen's five seconds (decision 0008, until T48). It stays across a change of tab (the bar stays mounted); when the bar goes (its line tapped, a screen over the tabs, the walk stopped) the discarded memo is let go, as the walk screen's is when it closes.
10. **BC10** (the bar's words; approved Nov 22) The bar's words (a refusal, "Hold to stop", "Too short, not kept", a save that failed) take a line of their own across the bar, above its buttons, whole at 320 px; the buttons stay where they are and the time stays in its line. Above the bar's buttons, top to bottom: a reminder (as today), then the words; the Undo pill sits above the bar on the page's ground. None gives way to another.

## Every surface (decision 0167)

- The recording bar is one component (`components/RecordingBar`), mounted once by the tabs' layout (`app/(tabs)/_layout.tsx`) while a walk is minimized: it sits over Explore and every section of Chronicle. Navigate is never the focused tab (it opens the walk screen), so the bar is never seen over it. On Memories the journal's open line files into the walk (decision 0112), so the bar keeps no capture there (BC1).
- The note's page (`NewNotePage`) opened from the bar is the one the walk screen's and Navigate's pencil open; its other hosts are unchanged.
